# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Clothing Color Match Studio to be a welcoming, constructive, and technically rigorous open-source project. Contributors, users, and maintainers are expected to treat one another with respect regardless of experience level, background, identity, location, or role.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive participation include:

- giving specific, actionable technical feedback
- assuming good faith while still challenging ideas on their merits
- documenting limitations and uncertainty honestly
- respecting privacy, licensing, and security boundaries
- helping newcomers understand the project's development and validation workflow
- keeping issues and pull requests focused on the project

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- knowingly submitting secrets, credentials, private customer images, or unlawfully obtained data
- deliberately misleading maintainers about test results, provenance, licensing, or security impact
- repeated disruptive behavior after a maintainer has asked for it to stop

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and conversations that violate this Code of Conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the repository maintainer when public disclosure would expose personal or security-sensitive information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces such as issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other interactions undertaken on behalf of the project.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and is informed by common open-source community standards. It may be expanded as the contributor community grows.
